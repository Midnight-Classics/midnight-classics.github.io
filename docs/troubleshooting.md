# Troubleshooting

## Nothing happens when I press CAL

The module is probably not getting power.

- **On USB-C:** check the cable is pushed fully into the socket, and that the charger, power bank or PC is on. Try
  another charger or cable.
- **On the car:** check the key is on, and that the plug is pushed fully into the pigtail's connector. Check the
  red wire goes to a 12 V feed that is on with the key, and the black wire goes to ground. Check the fuse in the red
  wire's fuse holder.

## The needle does not rest on 0

Fit the needle again. Lift it off with a fork, press CAL once and wait a second, push the needle on between the 0
and 5 marks, then press CAL again. See [Fitting the needle](needle.md).

## The needle stays at 0 with the engine running

The gauge is not getting a tach signal. When no signal arrives, it shows 0.

- Check the yellow wire is connected to one of the sources on the [Wiring](wiring.md) page.
- The gauge does not read an alternator's W terminal or a raw sensor pickup.

## The needle moves, but the reading is too high or too low

Check the cylinder switch on the back of the module matches your engine: 4, 6 or 8. On a Sprite it is 4. See step 9
in the manual.
