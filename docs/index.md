# Digital Smiths Tach Conversion

This kit replaces the mechanical movement inside the Smiths tachometer of a 1959 Austin Sprite with an electronic
module. You keep your own case, bezels, glass, faceplate and needle, so the gauge looks original. The module drops
into the case where the old mechanism was, and three wires connect it to the car. The old mechanism is not thrown
away: you bag it and keep it.
